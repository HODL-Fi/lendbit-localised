## Risk-parameter rationale

The liquidation bonus is up to 10%, so bad debt begins when `LT × 1.10` exceeds collateral value after the price gap between threshold-crossing and liquidation execution. Parameters are sized accordingly:

| Asset class | LTV / LT | Buffer to par | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stablecoins | 8500 / 9000 | 10 pts | negligible gap risk |
| ETH (both chains) | 8000 / 8500 | 15 pts | absorbs 10% bonus + ~4% adverse move |
| BNB | 7500 / 8300 | 17 pts | fatter historical tails than ETH |

<Warning>
Invariants enforced on-chain: `LTV ≥ 1000`, `LTV ≤ effective liquidation threshold` (both directions, at registration and on every setter), `threshold ≤ 10000`. An LTV equal to its threshold — max-borrow positions instantly at the liquidation boundary — was a live misconfiguration on BSC (USDC at 9000/9000) and has been corrected; the setter permits equality, so flag any configuration that recreates it.
</Warning>
