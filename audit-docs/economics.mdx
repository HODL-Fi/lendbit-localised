## Fixed-APR model

HODL uses a **single protocol-wide fixed APR** (`s_interestRate`, basis points), not a utilization curve. The former `LibInterestRateModel` utilization-curve code was removed as dead code. Governance sets the rate via `setInterestRate(rate, penaltyRate)`, which also propagates it to every deployed vault so LP accrual tracks borrower pricing.

Current live parameters (both chains):

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Borrower APR | 2245 bps (22.45%) |
| Penalty rate (post-maturity) | 500 bps |
| Utilization cap | strict `< 90%` (`MAX_UTILIZATION = 9000`) |
| Reserve factor (future vaults) | 100 bps (1% of interest revenue) |

## How LP yield derives from borrower APR

Interest accrues on **borrows** at the APR; LPs receive it net of the reserve factor, spread across **all deposits**. At maximum utilization:

```text
LP APY = APR × utilization × (1 − reserveFactor)
       = 22.45% × 0.90 × 0.99
       = 20.0%
```

The protocol's share is `APR × utilization × reserveFactor` ≈ 0.2% of deposits per year at the current 1% reserve factor.

<Note>
The 20% LP APY is realized only **at the utilization ceiling**. Below the cap LPs earn proportionally less; 100% utilization is disallowed by design, so up to ~10% of deposited capital is always idle — that is the exit-liquidity buffer.
</Note>

## Two accrual legs

| Leg | Rate source | Governance rate change |
|---|---|---|
| Floating (pooled) borrows | mutable vault `interestRate` | re-prices the borrower's **entire elapsed interval** at the new rate |
| Fixed-tenure loans | per-loan snapshotted `annualRateBps` via `fixedRateProduct` | immune |

<Warning>
Two set-once-parameter assumptions are load-bearing and documented as accepted risks:

1. **Do not cut a live rate while floating borrows are open.** The vault accrues the old (higher) rate for the elapsed interval, while borrower debt re-prices at the new (lower) rate — the gap becomes an LP receivable no borrower will pay, over-booking `totalAssets()` and penalizing the last LPs to redeem.
2. **Do not change the reserve factor while interest is accrued but unrepaid.** Accrual splits at the factor in effect per interval, but repayment re-splits the entire `interestPaid` at the current factor, drifting `totalAccruedInterest` and `totalProtocolReserve` from cash.

Both trace to the same root cause (no per-position borrow-index checkpoint). See [Known issues](/audit/known-issues).
</Warning>

## Penalty on tenured loans

Fixed-tenure loans accrue their snapshotted rate over the tenure, then the 500 bps penalty rate applies post-maturity. Outstanding balance = principal + tenure interest + post-maturity penalty.

## Auditor checkpoints

- Verify borrow capacity and utilization use consistent total-deposit and total-borrow bases across the diamond (`s_tokenVaultConfig`) and the vault (`totalBorrows`).
- Verify `_pendingInterest` nets the reserve factor at accrual while `_doRepay` realizes the protocol slice at repayment — the split must conserve cash.
- Verify fixed loans never accrue at the mutable rate (the `fixedRateProduct` separation).
- The interest math floors on division: check rounding direction favors the protocol on debt (round up) and LPs are not overpaid on accrual (round down).
